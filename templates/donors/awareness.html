Before You Become a Lifesaver

Every blood donation can help save a life. Please read the following information before registering.

Why Donate Blood?

  • Blood donation can help patients during accidents, surgeries and emergencies.
  • Donated blood supports patients with different medical conditions.
  • Eligible donors can donate regularly following appropriate medical guidance.

Important Information

  • Blood donation must be performed at an authorized blood donation centre.
  • Your eligibility will be assessed by qualified medical professionals.
  • Do not donate if you are unwell or have been advised against donating.
  • Always provide accurate personal and medical information.
  • Your contact information should be handled according to the platform's privacy practices.
